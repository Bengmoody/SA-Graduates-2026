Scenario 4 - Prep: management-group-scope deployment command
==============================================================

The reader/, contrib/, and owner/ folders here each deploy an Azure RBAC
role assignment at MANAGEMENT GROUP scope - not resource group or
subscription scope, like the rest of this repo - so they need a
different connect + deploy command.

1. Connect to Azure:

    Connect-AzAccount

2. Deploy (run from inside the reader/, contrib/, or owner/ folder):

    New-AzManagementGroupDeployment `
      -ManagementGroupId '<your-management-group-id>' `
      -Location 'uksouth' `
      -TemplateFile main.bicep `
      -TemplateParameterFile main.bicepparam

Notes:
- -ManagementGroupId is the management group's *name* (its ID), not its
  display name - find it with Get-AzManagementGroup.
- -Location is required by the deployment engine to store deployment
  metadata, even though no regional resources are created by any of
  these templates.
- You'll need enough Azure RBAC permission at that management group
  (at least "User Access Administrator", or "Owner") to create role
  assignments there - your everyday sign-in almost certainly won't have
  this by default, which is rather the point of the exercise.
- Remember to remove the role assignment afterwards if this was just for
  the lab:

    Remove-AzRoleAssignment -ObjectId <groupObjectId> -RoleDefinitionName '<Reader|Contributor|Owner>' -Scope '/providers/Microsoft.Management/managementGroups/<your-management-group-id>'
